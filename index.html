Professional Self-Assessment

My time at SNHU in the computer science program and developing this ePortfolio has expanded my coding knowledge and my confidence working on projects. Every project I worked on taught me something different about how systems work, how to make them better, and how to apply what I’ve learned in real situations. I have been able to apply the things I have learned at SNHU to my current job as a Desktop Support Technician. Even though I plan to stay on the IT infrastructure side of the field. This program helped me build a stronger technical foundation that makes me better at problem solving, supporting software teams, and understanding how everything connects from the database to the user interface.

Working through these projects gave me a lot of hands on experience that directly applies to teamwork and communication. I learned how important it is to follow best practices. This includes writing code that others can understand, use clear documentation, and keep systems consistent across a project. In my current job I collaborate with developers who use tools like DBeaver, VSCode, Node, and many more. So getting more familiar with it through my various projects really tied school and work together. It also showed me how much easier it is to talk with developers and database admins when you understand their tools and methods. Communicating technical ideas to both technical and non-technical people has been one of the most valuable skills I’ve built through this program. The program also strengthened my understanding of security and good coding practices. Whether it was validating input in Java or managing safe database connections in Python. I learned how to protect data and design systems that are both efficient and secure. I apply this daily at my job to keep our customers data safe.

All three artifacts together show a full range of my computer science skills. The Inventory App highlights my software engineering and UI abilities, the Grazioso Salvare CRUD module shows my knowledge of algorithms and data structures, and the DBeaver project demonstrates my database management and SQL skills. Together they form a complete picture of my growth through the program and how I can apply what I’ve learned to real technical environments. This ePortfolio not only represents the projects I’ve completed but also how I’ve evolved into a more confident and capable IT professional ready to support, design, and improve systems in any environment.
